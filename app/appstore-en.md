**Notes** (App Review, English)
```
Imperium: Laws & Nations is a grand strategy game about running a country. The game is in Swedish only (the App Store page says so). No login or account is needed.

How to play: tap ERÖVRA VÄRLDEN (Conquer the world), tap any country on the globe, tap GÖR TILL HEMLAND (Make home country) and pick a faction. NATION opens the country panel (laws, economy, research, media, agencies); STÄDER & BYGGNADER builds in your cities. Events appear on their own after a few in-game days.

In-app purchases: BUTIK (Shop) in the main menu, or the cart button in the bottom bar during a game. "Ingen reklam" (No Ads) is non-consumable and removes all ads; ÅTERSTÄLL KÖP (Restore purchases) restores it. "Statskassan", "Stora statskassan" and "Forskningsanslag" are consumables that add in-game money or research points to the single-player game that is running – please start a single-player game first; the amount is shown before purchase. Nothing is random.

Ads: Google AdMob interstitials, only in single-player, at most once every three minutes and only after the player closes an event. The app shows Google's consent form (UMP) where required and the App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads are requested.

Multiplayer: SPELA MED KOMPISAR (Play with friends) → SKAPA RUM (Create room) gives a four-character code; a second device joins with GÅ MED I RUMMET. Connections are peer-to-peer (WebRTC via the PeerJS signalling server). Only the name the player types is shared, and only with the players in the room.

Content: a political simulation. Historical ideologies – from democracies to dictatorships – are abstract choices with game effects; the game contains no extremist symbols and does not glorify any ideology. Leaders are historical figures shown with positive bonuses. Map data from Natural Earth (public domain), flags are public-domain national flags, tiles from Kenney (CC0), font Press Start 2P (OFL).

Contact: hello@8bitcat.io
```
